how one trade happens

numbers for decisions come from the apis. the phones are his hands.

    Every 60 seconds he pulls trending pump.fun coins from GMGN and drops rugs, bundles and dead volume.

  2. scope02

    Opens the coin on gmgn.ai so you see the same chart and holders he is reading.

  3. x check03

    Reads the project's X account: how old, how many followers, whether it is really about this coin.

  4. decidellm

    Scores the signals against his threshold. Skipping a coin above it needs a named reason.

  5. trade01

    Buys in the pump.fun app, checks the screen before confirming, leaves his thesis in the coin's comments.

  6. post03

    Posts the position on X. Exits run on their own: stop, take-profit, trailing, time stop.
